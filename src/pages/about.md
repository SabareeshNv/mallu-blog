Lone Voice is a personal notebook. It is where one person writes down ideas, thoughts and feelings, in Malayalam.

## What you will find here

There are two kinds of entry.

- **Essays** have a title. They are longer pieces, on memory, travel, reading and everyday life.
- **Scribbles** have no title. They are short notes, set down as they came.

You can read everything newest first on the [home page](/), browse by [topic](/topics/), or look through the [archive](/archive/), which lists every entry year by year.

## Language

The writing is in Malayalam. The pages around it are in English, so that anyone can find their way.

## Following along

There are no accounts, comments or newsletters here. To follow new entries, add the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) to a feed reader; most readers will find it from the address of this site.
